About Wasabi Meadow

A small, independent home-cooking site celebrating everyday Japanese food, seasonal ingredients and simple, thoughtful meals.

Our story

Why a meadow?

Japanese home cooking is less about elaborate technique and more about paying attention: to the season, to good rice, to a well-made broth and to the colour of the food on the table. We named this site after that green, gentle way of eating.

Wasabi Meadow shares recipes and kitchen knowledge for people who want to cook Japanese-inspired meals at home, wherever they live. We focus on dishes you can make on a weeknight with ingredients from a supermarket or Asian grocery.

Seasonal

We plan around what’s fresh and share an ingredient of the month.

Tested

Recipes are cooked in ordinary home kitchens before they’re published.

Respectful

We explain the traditions behind dishes and call our versions home-style.

Independent

We sell nothing and are not paid to feature any brand or product.

How we work

Clear, careful and practical

Each recipe gives quantities in grams and millilitres, clear timings and notes on common allergens such as soy, wheat, fish and sesame. Where a dish has a vegan or vegetarian version, we say so.

Our interactive tools, the bento builder and the rice and dashi calculator, run in your browser and are designed to make planning meals a little easier. You can read more in our Editorial Policy.
